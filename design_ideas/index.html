Design exploration

Playful directions for Shelf Showdown

The live app leans Netflix-dark and serious. These four concepts keep the same product job — pick a winner in a matchup — but make the moment feel more like a game, a zine, or a night at the bookstore.

Pick a direction to build next, or mix pieces. Nothing here is wired to the app yet.

01

Ring Round

Carnival bout · poster ink · mustard & brick

Lean into the “showdown” name. Every pick is a bout under the lights — chunky poster type, a stamped VS, and book titles that feel like fighter names on a card.

  • Hero brand is the whole first read
  • Matchup cards tilt like corner posters
  • Mustard / brick / off-white — no purple, no neon glow
  • Motion: posters slap in, VS stamps down

02

Stack Attack

Tabletop game night · felt green · chunky tokens

Treat the shelf like a board game. Soft felt play area, thick “token” book choices, and a progress chip that counts picks like turns.

  • Felt table texture + paper card stock
  • Lilita / Outfit — toy-store energy without cartoon chaos
  • Teal felt, coral token, cream cards
  • Motion: cards deal from a deck, bounce on pick

03

Zine Fight

Photocopied DIY · highlighter slash · crooked cutouts

A reader’s zine about taste. High-contrast ink, a chartreuse highlighter bar, and matchup panels that look scissor-cut and taped down.

  • Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono for stamps and captions
  • Newsprint grain, slight rotation, tape corners
  • Ink black, highlighter yellow-green, tomato accent
  • Motion: cutouts flutter in; highlighter draws under brand

04

Late Fee

Playful library desk · stamp pad · seafoam & ink

Warm checkout-desk humor without going full nostalgia costume. Rubber-stamp rank badges, due-date progress, and soft seafoam walls that still feel like a product — not a museum.

  • Fraunces for brand, DM Sans for UI
  • Seafoam field, ink navy type, stamp coral
  • Matchup as two “checkout cards”
  • Motion: stamp thump on pick, date stamp rolls
