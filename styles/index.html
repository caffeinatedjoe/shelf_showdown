Handful

Five books · drag to order · one submit

Rank a group of five in one turn: drag titles into best → worst order, then lock it in. Each screen captures a full relative ranking (~log₂(5!) bits) instead of a single pairwise pick.

  • Interactive: drag any row to reorder the stack
  • Rank badges update live as books move
  • Submit locks the handful and deals the next five
  • Best at the top — relative order only, no scores on this screen

    Soft Browse

    Layered glass · overlapping type · depth through opacity

    Two jackets float in frosted panels — stacked with soft overlap, ghost numerals, and blur so the matchup feels dimensional. Tiny chrome; rankings and library stay in the hamburger.

    • Navy gradient field with soft color washes behind the cards
    • Glass cards overlap slightly; large faded numerals sit behind titles
    • Covers keep a natural 2∶3 jacket ratio inside translucent panels
    • Hamburger menu: Rankings · Stats · Library · Sign out
    • Swipe left to undo, swipe right to skip — no buttons
