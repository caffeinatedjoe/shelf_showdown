Face Out

Phone shelf · jacket art first · one-thumb picks

A native-feeling sort screen: big covers you tap, title under each jacket, progress in the top bar, Skip/Undo in the thumb zone, tabs along the bottom.

  • Phone chrome + home indicator — reads as an app, not a site
  • Covers fill the screen; title/author never cover the art
  • Bottom tab bar: Sort · Rankings · Stats · Library
  • Motion: cover lifts toward you on press
