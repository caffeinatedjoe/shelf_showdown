Soft Browse

Pastel travel-app · rounded covers · menu tucked away

Two big jackets. Tiny chrome. Rankings and library sit in the hamburger — no tab bar, no chip row, no tutorial prompt.

  • Matchup fills ~80% of the screen
  • Covers stacked vertically at a natural 2∶3 jacket ratio
  • Hamburger menu: Rankings · Stats · Library · Sign out
  • Swipe left to undo, swipe right to skip — no buttons
  • Progress as a small counter only
